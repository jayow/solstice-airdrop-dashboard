System total at end date is pre-projected from Solstice's officially published daily delta (7-day average) — same source the dashboard's "Solstice (official)" chart uses. Changing the end date refreshes the projection. The "S2 SLX allocation" dropdown picks the campaign share — current docs anchor is 3% (30M SLX), with rumored bumps to 3.25% / 3.5% if Dynamic Distribution kicks in.

S2 loyalty multiplier (1.4x): Solstice applies a final multiplier once at end of S2 based on your retained TVL vs your S1 TWA baseline. Full Loyalty (TVL ≥ 100% of TWA) = 1.4x · Reduced (50-99%) = 1.3x · Minimum (20-49%) = 1.2x · Below 20% = 1.0x. If everyone receives the same multiplier, it cancels in the share ratio (your_flares ÷ total_flares), so SLX projection is unchanged. If you maintain 1.4x but average loyalty across all participants is lower (say 1.2x), your relative share — and SLX — would be roughly 1.4 ÷ 1.2 ≈ 17% higher than shown.
