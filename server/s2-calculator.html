About these numbers

System total at end date is pre-projected from Solstice's officially published daily delta (7-day average) — same source the dashboard's "Solstice (official)" chart uses. Changing the end date refreshes the projection.

The "S2 SLX allocation" picker selects the campaign share — current docs anchor is 3% (30M SLX), with rumored bumps to 3.25% / 3.5% if Dynamic Distribution kicks in.

S2 loyalty multiplier (1.4×): Solstice applies a final multiplier once at end of S2 based on your retained TVL vs your S1 TWA baseline. Full Loyalty (TVL ≥ 100% of TWA) = 1.4× · Reduced (50–99%) = 1.3× · Minimum (20–49%) = 1.2× · Below 20% = 1.0×. If everyone receives the same multiplier, it cancels in the share ratio, so SLX projection is unchanged.
